## What I do

I build tools that cut noise for defenders. Most of my work takes a flood of alerts, CVEs or suspicious emails and turns it into a short, ranked list of things a team can actually act on.

I came into security through law enforcement rather than a lab. As a Cyber Security Intern at Maharashtra Cyber I worked alongside analysts and investigators on digital forensics, cyber crime investigations, evidence analysis and threat intelligence workflows. Watching technical findings turn into real casework is what pushed me towards SOC operations, incident response and forensics instead of purely offensive work.

I am now finishing an MSc in Cyber Security at the University of Kent, and I am looking for SOC Analyst, Security Analyst, DFIR and Threat Intelligence roles in the UK.
